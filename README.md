# Field Kit — Electrician's Calculator

The electrical calculations you make every day — in one fast, clean app built for the new iPhone Duo.

Field Kit puts the electrician's most-used calculations in your pocket. No paper books, no clunky handhelds, no subscription — just ten precise tools with code-aware results, ready one-handed on the job site.

## Tools

- **Wire Sizing** — smallest conductor for your load, with continuous-load, temperature and bundling corrections applied
- **Voltage Drop** — 1φ and 3φ, copper or aluminum, instant verdict against the 3% / 5% recommendations
- **Conduit Fill** — percent fill by conductor mix, plus the smallest trade size that passes
- **Box Fill** — cubic-inch method check and the smallest standard box that fits
- **Load Calculation** — whole-house service sizing, standard method, line-by-line
- **Ohm's Law** — the full V · I · R · P wheel
- **Motor FLA** — full-load amps by horsepower, voltage and phase
- **Transformer** — kVA to amps, single- and three-phase
- **Grounding** — GEC and EGC conductor sizing
- **Ampacity Table** — the 75 °C table, browsable, with overcurrent caps

## Designed for iPhone Duo

Open Field Kit on the unfolded Duo and every tool sits beside its explanation, split at the hinge. Fold it and your calculator follows you to the cover screen.

## No subscription

Pay once. Every tool, every future update. No recurring fees — a tool should be an investment, not a rent.

## Verified data

Every table in Field Kit was cross-checked against published sources before release — and the app states which corrections apply where. Nothing hidden, nothing guessed.

## Disclaimer

Field Kit is an educational and reference tool. Always verify results against the current National Electrical Code and your local amendments.
