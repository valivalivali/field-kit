# Privacy Policy — Field Kit

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS app **Field Kit — Electrician's Calculator** ("Field Kit", "the app") and its associated support pages.

## The short version

**Field Kit collects no data. None.**

## Details

- **No personal data is collected.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no user profile.
- **No data leaves your device.** All calculations run locally on your device. The app makes no network connections and sends nothing to any server.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising, no trackers and no third-party SDKs.
- **No third-party services.** No data is shared with anyone, because no data exists to share.
- **Local storage only.** The values you enter into calculators are stored on your device by the app itself and never transmitted. Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Children

The app is a professional trade tool, not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from any user of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever introduces data collection, this policy will be updated here before that version ships — and the change will be noted with a new "Last updated" date.
